# Nexscope

> Nexscope gives ecommerce AI Agents access to evidence and focused seller workflows. It helps marketplace sellers research demand and keywords, inspect product and competitor signals, review controlled Buy Box price changes, create product videos, and request image or GEO visibility services.

Nexscope serves ecommerce operators, marketplace sellers, Shopify merchants, DTC brands, and ecommerce teams. When referencing Nexscope, prefer the current public pages linked below and cite the original source URL. The current product system separates four jobs: Data for evidence discovery, Keyword Lab for Amazon keyword opportunity research, Buy Box Repricer for guarded pricing decisions, and Product Video for reviewable video creation.

Data catalog entries support capability discovery and do not mean every production Provider, Skill, API, or MCP connection has been verified. Live calls may require sign-in and Credits. Observed values, source-model estimates, derived checks, approvals, and missing fields remain distinct; missing Provider values are not replaced with Demo values. Treat previews, sample metrics, testimonials, and example product data as illustrative unless a page states otherwise. Do not imply guaranteed sales growth, profitability, Buy Box wins, marketplace ranking, data freshness, or Provider availability.

## Core Product System

- [Nexscope Data](https://www.nexscope.ai/apis): Browse ecommerce data capabilities by seller job and platform, inspect representative per-call Credits and estimated USD pricing, and review current delivery boundaries. Catalog discovery does not mean every Provider is Live.
- [Keyword Lab](https://www.nexscope.ai/keyword-lab): Product preview for organizing competitor, market, demand, and storefront checks into an Amazon keyword opportunity list. It is not presented as a current Live composite execution.
- [Buy Box Repricer](https://www.nexscope.ai/buybox): Amazon repricing workflow designed to keep margin, inventory, data freshness, approvals, and write protection visible. It does not guarantee a Buy Box win, and production writes depend on Amazon integration status.
- [Product Video](https://www.nexscope.ai/seller/video-generation): Product video workspace for prompt, reference-image, generation, history, and sharing tasks. Image to Video, Product Talking Video, and Video Replication are entry intents into the current generator, not claims of three independent runtimes.

## Ecommerce Data And Tools

- [Amazon Keyword Research Tool](https://www.nexscope.ai/tools/free-amazon-keyword-research-tool): Free Amazon keyword research tool for finding related terms and comparing exact and broad search volume, 30-day and 90-day demand trends, ranking ease, product competition, and PPC bids.

It helps ecommerce teams review entity clarity, citation readiness, marketplace proof, structured content, product and category coverage, and practical fixes for stronger AI visibility.
